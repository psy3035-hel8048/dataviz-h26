## Preparation for next week
We will hear from those that haven't presented yet. I'm not sure how long this will take but, if time allows, I would like to use the rest of our time to have an open discussion about use of AI. I know many of us are probably getting sick and tired of talking about this theme, but I believe this class is an especially good place to think a bit more deeply about it. AI is revolutionising what it means to be a programmer - even for those of us that learned coding pre-AI. But we still need to work out how we integrate it into our workflow, what are the costs associated with its use, and how do we report and use it in an ethical way.

The only homework then, apart from to continue working on your projects, is to think a bit about this topic and to read one or both of these articles.

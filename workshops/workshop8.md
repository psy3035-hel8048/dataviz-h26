## Follow up

### Lambda functions
We often use `lambda` functions to convert or to create new columns when we are cleaning our data. This is normally in combination with `.assign`.

These functions - also called anonymous functions - are used a lot in Python but can be a bit tricky to get your head around. They work really well in this case though when you want to create a simple function (e.g., to transform your data) using a single line of code.
